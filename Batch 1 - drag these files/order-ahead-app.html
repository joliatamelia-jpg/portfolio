*This was a user-experience exercise, not a branding one. The visual treatment is deliberately plain. The goal of this project was to walk through a typical user experience ordering from this cafe. 

01 — The flow

Wired before it was styled

Every screen was connected in Adobe XD before any of it was decorated. Browse is the hub, product cards open detail, the icons on each card fire their own confirmations, and the tab bar reaches cart and account from anywhere. 

02 — The mechanic

One idea, carried across five screens

The loyalty currency is the spine of the experience, and it appears at every point where a customer would want to know about it. Like many other food apps, a loyalty program keeps customers coming back to use their points. 

Before
The add-to-cart confirmation tells you what the item will earn, so the incentive lands at the moment of choice.
During
Cart and checkout both show the balance applied as a discount line, next to the subtotal it reduces.
After
The confirmation, the order tracker, and the account screen each restate what was earned and what the balance is now.

The product detail screen carries another important variety of details: allergens, ingredients, and a live count of what's left. A bakery sells out, so stock should be visible before someone commits to a pickup time.

04 — Where it stops

If this project continued

This prototype is a project from when I was a student, so I never finished building out the full app due to the time constraint and project requirements. I was not thorough in a completed app, because the goal was only to show the flow of purchasing an item. If I were to pick it up now, here are some things I would add... 

Favorites/Favs
The heart icon and its confirmation both exist, so the saved-items list is the obvious next screen. App notifications would be user specific based on favorited products.
Rewards catalog
The account screen shows a balance and a "view rewards" link with nothing behind it yet. The finished concept of the loyalty program would include actual rewards and a way to activate them. 
Search & categories
The field and the category chips are designed but not wired to result states; the concept would be the same throughout. 
Sign up & menu
Account creation and the drawer behind the hamburger are both stubs. 
Real catalog copy
The browse grid repeats one placeholder product. A populated menu would be an important upgrade. 
Reconciled totals
Cart and confirmation currently disagree on the discount. Real numbers would come from a defined earn rate.
