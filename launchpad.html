The process
01

Brand research & survey review

Reviewed the company's new website, took note of colors and icons. Reviewed the information from the survey that was conducted, prepared for initial design.

02

Editorial spread design

Designed two similar, yet different looks for the first deliverable (the spread) for the client to choose from. Combination of custom icons and stock.
The client decided on version one, which inspired the colorful design of the final e-book. 

03

Rework into zine

Expanded upon the approved design concept to create an 8-page zine and e-book based on the original spread. Two final versions were delivered for both printing and digital purposes. View the full project below. 

04

Marketing campaign

Used the established design to create cohesive marketing materials for social media and HTML web ads following IAB standard sizes. View the live HTML ads in the gallery below. 

Final work
Digital campaign

The full ad set built in live HTML, produced in a light and a dark treatment for placement flexibility.
