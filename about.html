Biography

Amelia Joliat is a full-stack graphic designer with a passion for delivering polished work.

While Amelia earned her Bachelor's in Visual Communication Design from Kent State University in 2024, her passion for design stems from the computer games she played in her youth.

Completing her minor in User Experience Design, Amelia learned essential HTML and CSS skills to pair with her extensive knowledge and experience using the Adobe Creative Suite.

These skills have been honed during her years at North Coast Media (NCM), a Cleveland-based B2B publishing company. Amelia worked directly with clients to deliver custom static digital marketing materials, animated HTML ads (NCM's first!), and printed editorial pieces for a wide range of companies. She has experience working both independently and as a key component of a talented team.
