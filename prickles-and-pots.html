Prickles & Pots

An unofficial rebrand of Succulent Garden Gifts, a family-run nursery in Milford, Ohio. Renamed, rebranded, and carried from the wordmark through to a working storefront.

Subject
Succulent Garden Gifts
Milford, OH
Role
Identity, illustration, UI
Context
Kent State Capstone
Year
2024
01 — The mark

Initial Logo Concepting

Every element of the identity begins as a painted illustration created in Illustrator (vector format) with watercolor-style brushes for a natural feel. The wordmark is set in Lullabies, a brush face chosen to match the painted strokes, so the type and the plants read as one object rather than a logo placed above an image.  The name came first: Succulent Garden Gifts described the inventory, but said nothing about the handmade pots the shop is actually known for. Prickles & Pots does.

02 — The system

One pot becomes the pattern

The painted texture on the pots is lifted out and tiled into a repeat, which then does the work of a secondary graphic: trimming the tote, wrapping the planter, capping the business card, and adding depth where it is needed. 

03 — The storefront

Mapped, then built

The site was wire-framed in Figma before it was styled: three top-level pages, a shop that splits seventeen products across cedar, ceramic, and glass, and a purchase path running product detail, add to cart, cart.  Every product, price, and photograph on the storefront is the shop's real inventory.

04 — Try it

The working prototype

Click through it the way a customer would. The nav, the Shop now buttons, the product cards, add to cart, and the cart itself are all live. This was originally prototyped in Adobe XD, a now discontinued software. I salvaged what I could and reproduced it here in HTML. The checkout page was missing.
